Working

Required socket length is the largest of the geotechnical length, the NYC BC 1812.7.3 minimum (3 ft or the casing OD, whichever is greater), the stud length (rows × row spacing) and the threadbar development length, each rounded up. Geotechnical length = (load × FS) ÷ (ultimate skin friction × π × socket diameter), plus any length ignored at the top of rock. End bearing is off by default. If it is turned on, the allowable end bearing is subtracted from the compression load before sizing.
